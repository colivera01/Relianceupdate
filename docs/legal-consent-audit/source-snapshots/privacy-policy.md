## Current user-visible clauses

The page is titled **Privacy Policy** and states that Reliance collects:

- account information, including name, email, phone, profile details, and security settings;
- service and booking details, including customer/vendor relationships, service types, dates, work records, and status;
- consent records, including acceptance status, timestamps, version references, and related history;
- service media, including before, during, and after videos and associated metadata;
- device, browser, IP, security, verification, communications, review, activity, moderation, Trust Score, and public-proof information.

The page says information may be used to operate accounts and dashboards, manage booking and consent workflows, deliver and moderate service videos, maintain compliance logs and disputes, send operational notifications, and calculate Trust Score or public proof.

The sharing clause permits sharing with vendors and customers as needed for a service relationship and with service providers that support hosting, communications, analytics, moderation, security, and platform operations.

The SMS clause describes a transactional program, says message frequency varies, provides STOP and HELP instructions, and says mobile information is not sold or shared for third-party marketing.

The media clause says service media and consent records may be retained for compliance, support, security, dispute handling, and product operations. It says approved public media may appear on vendor profiles, public service pages, and related proof experiences.

The page says AI may assist with triage and recommendations, but final administrative decisions remain with authorized Reliance personnel.

The retention clause states:

> We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, meet legal and compliance obligations, preserve consent and moderation history, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

The rights clause says users may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable and that some records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, consent, moderation, or dispute purposes.
