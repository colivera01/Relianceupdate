In this tutorial, we will walk through how to launch your vendor profile on Reliance.

Your goal is simple: help customers understand who you are, what work you offer, and what proof they can trust.

Start with accurate business details. These details support your dashboard, public credibility profile, and approval review.

Your vendor dashboard should answer four questions. Am I visible? What can customers see? What increases customer trust? What should I do next?

Next, complete your public credibility profile. Your business photo, bio, category, specialties, and contact details shape the first impression customers see.

Then build your Services Offered menu. Think of each service as a clear menu item customers can understand before they request help from your business.

Saving a service offered does not automatically make it public. Approval and publishing stay separate so Reliance can keep public information clean and trustworthy.

As jobs are completed, approved service videos and customer reviews become public trust signals customers can compare.

To launch well, focus on three things first: a complete profile, a clear Services Offered menu, and completed work that can become public proof.
