## Explicit activation blockers

1. **Decide Later:** current Customer recording runtime still exposes a retired Decide Later action. Revision 2 does not include it and does not modify runtime. Remove/retire it only under separate authorization before new recording wording becomes effective, without rewriting historical evidence.
2. **Support contact:** the Product Owner-selected draft contact differs from the live canonical customer-support configuration. Align source/configuration before activation; this revision does not change either live address.
3. **Employee policy copy:** current Employee recording-participation policy remains marked counsel-review-required. Revision 2 does not claim counsel approval or activate Phase 3C.
4. **Provider inventory:** Privacy and Terms provider categories must be checked against the exact enabled provider/data-flow inventory before publication.
5. **Retention triggers/workers:** target periods do not define start events and are not live promises. No lifecycle worker, scheduler, or deletion action is enabled.
6. **Withdrawal/privacy cases:** dormant schema is not a live verified intake, staff workflow, dependency resolver, or outcome process.
7. **Public dependency removal:** engineering must prove that withdrawn/non-current required standing permission promptly holds/removes dependent Reliance-controlled Public display.
8. **SMS readiness:** Employee SMS verification remains disabled; this package does not enable it.

## Review gates before any effective version

- Product Owner approves each exact publishable item.
- Counsel approves, revises, or rejects provisions requiring legal review.
- Engineering completes mapped dependencies and proves source-to-runtime identity.
- Any post-review edit creates a new immutable version/hash.
- No historical evidence is relabeled, backfilled, or reinterpreted.
- Controlled beta acceptance verifies exact display, actor identity, durable copy, submit-time context, stored publishable snapshot/hash, support/withdrawal path, and accessibility.
- A separate authorization explicitly creates and deploys an effective version. A commit, manifest, or Product Owner review comment alone cannot activate content.
