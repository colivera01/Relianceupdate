# Privacy Request Intake

You may ask Reliance Technologies LLC about personal information associated with you. Depending on your identity, relationship, location, applicable law, and available exceptions, you may ask to access, correct, delete, or obtain information; withdraw a prospective consent; or dispute an attribution.

Use the authenticated Support & Help or privacy-request path when one is available to you. Former participants, accountless users, and anyone who cannot access that path may send a request to colivera080124@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, signed links, bearer tokens, or full payment credentials.

## Request type

- Access or explanation
- Correction
- Deletion
- Portable copy, where applicable
- Recording or Public participation withdrawal
- Public/Private visibility assistance
- Decision or identity dispute
- SMS help or opt-out assistance
- Accessibility assistance
- Other privacy question

Reliance requests only information reasonably needed to locate the relevant records and verify identity and authority. Depending on context, that may include your name, preferred contact method, role, Vendor, approximate service date, Work Record reference, and a description of the request. Do not provide a password, one-time code, signed URL, or unnecessary government identifier through an ordinary form.

## Verification

We may need to verify that you are the person concerned or are authorized to act for them before disclosing or changing information. Email address alone may not be enough for an Employee, Customer, or Vendor decision. A request submitted by an agent may require proof of authority. Former or reassigned Employees can use a verified path without reactivating Vendor membership.

## Receipt

**Title:** Request received

We received your request and created a reference number. We will review identity, authority, applicable law, and any evidence-preservation or legal-hold requirements before acting. We may contact you for additional verification. Do not send credentials in a reply.

## Outcome

Reliance will explain the action taken, information provided, correction recorded, deletion completed, or reason an item was retained or denied. Any exception will be described at a useful level without exposing another person's information, security controls, or privileged material.

## Timing

Reliance will respond within the time required by applicable law for the verified request. A single deadline does not apply to every requester or jurisdiction.
