# Withdrawal and Decision Dispute Notice

## Requesting help

To withdraw a prospective consent or participation choice, dispute a decision attributed to you, report coercion, or correct an identity or authority error, contact colivera080124@gmail.com. Do not include a password, one-time code, signed Service Order link, bearer token, or other credential.

Reliance may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity, role, authority, Vendor membership, Work Record relationship, or the decision at issue. If you no longer have an active account or Vendor membership, Reliance will provide a verified request path that does not require membership reactivation.

## What a verified withdrawal does

A verified withdrawal applies prospectively to the permission identified in the request:

- new recording that depends on withdrawn Customer authorization or Employee participation remains blocked;
- new Public publication that depends on withdrawn standing Public participation or Customer package authorization remains blocked;
- if current Reliance-controlled Public display depends on a permission that has been validly withdrawn or has become non-current, Reliance promptly holds or removes that controlled Public display after verifying and processing the request; and
- independently authorized Private Proof access may remain available.

Reliance preserves immutable historical decisions, audit and visibility history, reports, and the minimum evidence needed to understand what occurred. A legal hold, security need, dispute, or evidence-preservation duty may require Reliance to retain underlying records without keeping the content publicly displayed.

Withdrawal does not automatically erase historical evidence, undo processing already completed under a valid prior authorization, cancel an underlying real-world service, or retrieve copies made by others outside Reliance.

## Disputing a decision or attribution

You may dispute that you made a decision or that the recorded identity, authority, membership, assignment, scope, or content version was correct. Reliance will preserve the challenged evidence and separately record the request, review, actions, and outcome rather than overwrite history. A dispute is not automatically a finding that the original evidence is false.

## Coercion and retaliation

An Employee who believes a Vendor or Manager pressured or retaliated against them for a recording or Public choice may contact Reliance. Reliance will limit disclosure and provide human review. This notice does not replace rights under applicable law.

## Response

After verification, Reliance will provide a reference, explain the action taken or any additional verification needed, and identify applicable exceptions at a useful level without exposing another person's information, security controls, or privileged material. Response timing depends on the verified request and applicable law.
