# Reliance Privacy Notice

## 1. Scope and operator

This notice explains how Reliance Technologies LLC ("Reliance," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information in the Reliance platform. Questions or requests may be sent to `colivera080124@gmail.com`. This contact is not represented as a physical office, registered office, registered-agent address, or an address that is sufficient for every form of statutory or legal notice.

This notice covers Customers, Vendor representatives, Vendor Managers, assigned service professionals, accountless Employee Service Order users, visitors, and people whose authorized image or voice may appear in a Service Video. Additional just-in-time notices and exact decision text may apply to a particular recording, Public choice, or role.

## 2. Information we may collect

Depending on the feature and role, Reliance may process:

- account and identity information, such as name, email, phone, role, authentication state, and verified principal or membership identifiers;
- Vendor and workforce information, such as organization, membership, assignment, service role, and Service Order delivery or verification state;
- Customer and service information, such as contact details, service location, booking details, service type, schedule, lifecycle status, and ownership relationship;
- consent and decision evidence, such as exact text and version shown, decision value, timestamp, actor and context identifiers, verification method, immutable history, and cryptographic hashes;
- Service Video and media information, including video, authorized audio, stage, package, technical metadata, content status, audit outcome, visibility history, and reports;
- precise or approximate location evidence used for authorized recording-stage verification, including accuracy and timestamps where the workflow requires it;
- safety and workflow evidence, including pre-stage confirmations, recording gates, sessions, uploads, review state, and immutable evidence links;
- customer reviews, optional Employee ratings, Favorites, support messages, reports, dispute information, and privacy requests;
- notification and communications data, including delivery channel, destination metadata, provider status, opt-out state, and message evidence, but not raw one-time codes as consent proof;
- device, security, and usage information, such as IP address, browser or device information, request and correlation identifiers, logs, access events, fraud signals, and error diagnostics; and
- AI feature inputs, outputs, feedback, and audit metadata when an authorized AI-assisted feature is used.

Reliance does not intentionally collect minors, bystanders, unrelated private conversations, private documents, passwords, access codes, or security information in Service Videos. Users must stop or retake when those conditions arise.

## 3. Sources

We may receive information directly from you; from the Customer, Vendor, or assigned service professional involved in a Work Record; from verified accountless Service Order access; from devices used for an authorized workflow; from service providers used to operate that workflow; and from Reliance's own security, support, and audit operations.

One person's submission does not automatically establish authority to make another person's consent decision. Reliance uses separate role, identity, assignment, and context checks for consequential decisions.

## 4. Purposes

Reliance may use information to:

- create and manage accounts, Vendor relationships, assignments, bookings, and Service Orders;
- verify identity, authority, ownership, membership, and current assignment context;
- present and record role-specific assent, recording authorization, recording participation, and Public or Private choices;
- enforce current-context recording, safety, location, media, audit, and visibility controls;
- provide Private Proof and, only when authorized, Public visibility for an exact package;
- provide customer reviews, Favorites, reports, support, dispute handling, and privacy-request handling;
- send necessary transactional communications and optional transactional SMS where chosen and available;
- prevent fraud, investigate misuse, secure the platform, maintain logs, and preserve evidence;
- comply with law and protect rights, safety, and platform integrity;
- operate, troubleshoot, measure, maintain, and improve the Reliance features a person is authorized to use; and
- provide AI-assisted summaries, flags, or workflow help under human oversight.

Reliance does not use a recording authorization or job-specific Employee participation decision as permission for Public use. Reliance does not treat an Employee's standing Public participation decision as Customer authorization to publish an exact package.

## 5. Recording and audio

Reliance recording requires the applicable current Customer authorization, each required Employee's job-specific participation decision, and operational evidence required by the recording workflow. Audio is permitted only when the displayed scope includes audio and all legally required parties have provided the required authorization. When the location of relevant parties or applicable law is uncertain, Reliance may require authorization from all required parties before audio recording.

Precise location is collected only when the authorized workflow requires stage or job-site verification. It is not used for advertising, unrelated tracking, or Public display.

## 6. Public Service Videos

Approved Service Videos are Private by default. Public-display eligibility does not itself publish a package. A package becomes Public only after the authenticated owning Customer confirms the exact Public choice and all current package, participant, audit, media, and restriction requirements pass.

When Public, the complete authorized package and any authorized audio may be visible to anyone using Reliance's Public surfaces. Other people may copy, record, download, or redistribute Public content outside Reliance's control. Making a package Private stops new Public access through Reliance's controlled surfaces after the change succeeds, but it cannot guarantee deletion of outside copies. Reliance preserves prior authorization and visibility evidence.

## 7. Disclosure

Reliance may disclose information:

- among the Customer, Vendor, assigned service professional, and Reliance personnel to the minimum extent needed for the authorized workflow;
- to service providers used for cloud hosting, databases and storage, identity and security, maps or geocoding, email and optional messaging, and AI-assisted features, to the extent each provider is enabled for the relevant feature;
- on Public Reliance surfaces when all required Public authorizations are current;
- to investigate reports, protect users, enforce terms, or respond to a safety or security event;
- in a business transaction subject to appropriate protections; and
- when required or permitted by law.

Reliance does not claim that a provider never trains on, retains, or otherwise uses data unless Reliance has verified that exact commitment for the configured service.

## 8. AI-assisted processing

Reliance may use AI to help summarize, organize, classify, flag, or draft workflow information. Human reviewers remain responsible for consequential decisions. AI is not the final decision-maker for Reliance Audit, recording authorization, Public visibility, content reports, employment, workmanship, safety, licensure, code compliance, or legal rights.

Reliance does not secretly expand previously collected information to a materially new AI purpose without appropriate notice and, where required, affirmative consent.

## 9. Retention

Reliance retains information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described, including service delivery, evidence integrity, security, disputes, legal obligations, and enforcement. Intended operational targets are addressed in the separate retention policy and are not unconditional deletion guarantees.

The intended targets are approximately 12 months for Private media, a minimum of 7 years for minimum decision and evidence records, and approximately 30 days for raw precise-location values, subject to valid legal holds, reports, disputes, security needs, and other legal or contractual requirements. The exact lifecycle event that starts each period will be defined in the final versioned retention schedule after operational validation.

Public media may remain available while valid Public authorization continues. Deleting or withdrawing media does not necessarily erase minimum evidence needed to prove what was authorized, what occurred, and how Reliance responded.

## 10. Choices and requests

Subject to identity, authority, applicable law, and available exceptions, a person may ask to access, correct, delete, or obtain information; withdraw a prospective consent; dispute an attribution or decision; or ask how information was used. Requests may be sent to `colivera080124@gmail.com`.

Reliance may verify identity and authority before acting. Reliance responds within the time required by applicable law for the verified person and request. Email preferences and optional SMS choices can be managed through available controls or support. Essential security and transactional communications may still be sent where permitted.

## 11. Security

Reliance uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed for the nature of the information. Measures include role and context checks, current-state revalidation, cryptographic hashes, restricted credentials, evidence history, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, bearer tokens, signed URLs, or other secrets through support messages.

## 12. Children

Reliance is not designed for children to create accounts or make decisions. Minors must not be intentionally included in Service Video recording. If a minor or unrelated person enters the recording area, the recording must stop or be retaken under the approved safety workflow. This notice does not create a parent or guardian authorization flow.

## 13. International and state-specific rights

Reliance currently plans its operations around the United States and Florida. Privacy rights vary by location and may depend on thresholds and exemptions. Reliance evaluates verified requests under applicable law and preserves nonwaivable rights.

## 14. Changes to this notice

Reliance versions material changes, publishes or presents the updated notice, and obtains fresh affirmative assent or consent when required by the nature of the change. Continued use alone does not authorize a materially new processing or consent proposition. Historical evidence remains bound to the exact version shown at the time.

## 15. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, withdrawal, disputes, or accessibility help, contact `colivera080124@gmail.com`. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, signed Service Order links, or other credentials.
