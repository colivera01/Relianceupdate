# Customer Service Video Visibility Choices

## Private state

**Status:** Private

Your complete Service Video is visible only through authorized Private Proof access. Starting Condition, Work in Progress, and Final Result stay together as one exact package.

If the exact package is not eligible for Public display, show:

**Private only:** This Service Video is available as Private Proof but is not eligible for Public display.

## Share Publicly confirmation

**Title:** Make this Service Video public?

**Exact confirmation text:** Your complete Service Video - Starting Condition, Work in Progress, and Final Result - will become publicly viewable on Reliance after you confirm and the server verifies every current requirement. People outside Reliance may view, copy, screen-record, download, or redistribute Public content. Reliance cannot guarantee removal of copies made outside its systems.

When the package contains authorized audio, also show:

**Audio warning:** This Service Video contains audio. If you continue, the approved video and its authorized audio will become publicly viewable and may be copied or redistributed outside Reliance.

**Required checkbox:** I understand that I am making the complete exact Service Video Public and that copies made outside Reliance may remain even if I later make it Private.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Share Publicly

The server must revalidate the authenticated owning Customer, active Private Proof grant, exact Admin PASS, Public-display eligibility, exact package and all three stage identities/hashes, required participant permissions, media state, restrictions, and absence of a superseding package. Customer confirmation cannot override another person's required Public permission. If a required permission is pending, the package remains Private and the state must say Waiting for Public-sharing permission, not Public Review.

## Public state

**Status:** Public

Your complete Service Video is publicly viewable on Reliance.

This status appears only after the server transaction succeeds. Public-display eligibility alone does not create this state.

## Make Private confirmation

**Title:** Make this Service Video private?

**Exact confirmation text:** Reliance will remove this complete Service Video from its controlled Public surfaces after you confirm and the server completes the change. You will keep access through Private Proof. Copies previously made outside Reliance may remain, and Reliance cannot retrieve or delete those outside copies.

**Required checkbox:** I understand that Make Private stops new Public access through Reliance but may not remove copies already made elsewhere.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Make Private

## Republish

If the Customer later chooses Share Publicly again, Reliance must revalidate all current requirements. An earlier Customer authorization, participant permission, audit eligibility, or package state is not permanently valid.

## Evidence contract

Each consequential choice should bind the verified owning Customer, booking, exact package ID/version/hash, exact stage-set hash, choice, audio presence and disclosure, outside-copy disclosure, exact displayed content key/version/snapshot/hash, request evidence, timestamp, and immutable evidence hash. Historical visibility decisions remain preserved and must not be rewritten.

## Activation dependencies

- Attorney review of Public-display license and outside-copy disclosure.
- Exact package and disclosure evidence through the dormant Public-choice content model.
- Audio-specific confirmation when applicable.
- Participant-permission and restriction revalidation.
- Public Browse removal verification for Make Private.
- Historical visibility-contract compatibility.
