# Reliance Accessibility Statement

Reliance Technologies LLC wants Customers, Vendors, service professionals, and visitors to be able to use Reliance effectively. Our internal design and engineering target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA for customer-facing and role-critical experiences, including keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful labels, sufficient contrast, understandable errors, responsive layouts, captions or alternatives where applicable, and compatibility with common assistive technologies.

This target is not a claim that every current page or third-party component conforms, and Reliance is not presenting itself as independently certified. Accessibility is ongoing work that includes design review, automated checks, keyboard and screen-reader testing, mobile testing, and remediation of reported barriers.

## Ask for help or report a barrier

If you cannot access a Reliance feature or document, email `colivera080124@gmail.com`. Please describe the page or task, the barrier, your device or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing it, and a preferred way to receive help. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, signed links, or other credentials.

Reliance should acknowledge the request, provide an accessible alternative where reasonably possible, and track remediation without requiring a person to disclose unnecessary disability or medical information.

## Language access

Reliance may provide translated content for convenience. Consequential consent or assent should use a controlled, versioned translation process with review for meaning, a clear language identifier, and the same exact-snapshot evidence standard. Machine translation alone should not be treated as a verified legal translation for consequential decisions.

## Third-party content

Some third-party services or content may have separate accessibility limitations. Reliance should select and configure providers with accessibility in mind and provide a support path when a critical third-party component prevents access.

## Activation dependencies

- Baseline accessibility audit of role-critical routes and documents.
- Documented keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, error, mobile, and media checks.
- Accessible-alternative and issue-remediation workflow.
- Controlled translation governance for consequential text.
- Product Owner review of any conformance statement after evidence exists.
