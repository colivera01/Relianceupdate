# Accountless Employee Reliance Notice and Assent

## Display title

Before you use this Service Order

## Who and why

The Vendor shown on this Service Order identified you as the assigned service professional for this Work Record. Reliance Technologies LLC provides the Service Order and, when separately authorized, the recording workflow. This page is for your own verified Employee context. Do not use another person's link or make a decision for someone else.

## What this assent covers

By affirmatively agreeing, you acknowledge that:

1. You may use the current Service Order only for the assigned Work Record and Vendor shown.
2. The Service Order link is an entry credential and may require a one-time verification step. It may expire or become stale after reassignment or scope replacement.
3. You will protect the link, one-time code, and verified session and will not share them.
4. Reliance may process the minimum identity, Vendor membership, assignment, Service Order, security, and evidence information needed to verify your access and operate the workflow.
5. Any job-specific Recording Participation choice is separate and will be presented before recording. Accepting this accountless notice does not allow recording.
6. Standing Public Service Video Participation is also separate and optional. It is not required to open a Service Order or perform Private Proof work.
7. A Vendor or Manager cannot accept, decline, or override your personal recording or standing Public choice.
8. Reliance recording authorization does not certify workmanship, job-site safety, licensure, code compliance, or employment status.
9. You must stop or avoid recording if a minor, unrelated adult, bystander, private conversation, private document or screen, credential, access code, or security information could be captured.
10. You have access to the linked Terms and Privacy Notice and can retain a copy of the exact versions shown.

## Separate affirmative acknowledgments

**Identity checkbox:** I am the verified assigned person for this Service Order and am acting only for myself.

**Terms checkbox:** I agree to the exact Reliance Terms of Use version shown for my use of this accountless Service Order.

**Privacy checkbox:** I acknowledge that I reviewed the exact Reliance Privacy Notice version shown and understand the described processing for this Service Order.

**Electronic-record checkbox:** I consent to receive this notice, the Service Order, decision receipts, and related records electronically and can access and retain the versions shown.

**Button:** Agree and continue

All required acknowledgments must be selected before assent. Reliance should record each presented content key/version separately rather than infer assent from opening the link or completing OTP verification.

## Correction and decline actions

**Button:** This Service Order is not for me

Use this when the link was sent to the wrong person or you are not the assigned person. It is an identity or assignment correction, not a refusal to participate in recording and not acceptance of any term.

**Button:** I do not agree

Declining does not submit assent or a recording choice. Reliance should explain that the verified Service Order flow cannot continue and offer support. The Vendor must not treat a declined accountless-platform assent as the Employee's job-specific recording decision.

## Electronic-record notice

Selecting Agree and continue expresses intent to sign electronically. Reliance should provide access to a durable copy and record the verified Employee principal, Vendor membership, assignment and Service Order context, exact content key, version, text snapshot, hash, timestamp, and verification class. Raw one-time codes, tokens, and signed URLs must not be stored as assent evidence.

## Activation dependencies

- Strong verified Employee identity distinct from email address alone.
- Current Service Order release and assignment revalidation at display and submit.
- Durable-copy delivery and versioned Terms/Privacy references.
- No retaliation or proxy-assent path.
- Separate job-specific recording and standing Public surfaces.
