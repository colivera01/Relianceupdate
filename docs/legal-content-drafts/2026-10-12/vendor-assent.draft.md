> Not effective. Not counsel-approved. This draft is not connected to Vendor registration, sign-in, assignment, or Service Order delivery.

# Vendor and Vendor Manager Assent

## Display title

Vendor responsibilities and Reliance platform terms

## Introductory notice

You are accepting for the Vendor organization identified on this screen. By continuing, you confirm that you are authorized to bind that organization to the version shown and to receive this record electronically.

## Required acknowledgments

I confirm that:

1. I have authority to act for the identified Vendor organization.
2. The Vendor is responsible for the services it performs, its personnel, required licenses and insurance, workplace obligations, and the truthfulness of information it submits.
3. Reliance is a technology platform and does not certify workmanship, job-site safety, code compliance, licensure, or professional quality.
4. The Vendor will assign only the correct current Employee or service professional and will keep contact and assignment information accurate.
5. The Vendor will not accept or decline recording participation or standing Public participation for an Employee, and will not make a Customer's recording or Public-visibility choice.
6. The Vendor will not retaliate against, coerce, or misrepresent the decision of an Employee who declines optional Reliance recording or optional standing Public participation.
7. The Vendor will use the current Service Order and reassignment/scope-replacement process. A stale link, prior assignment, prior assessment, or prior Employee decision cannot authorize a current context.
8. The Vendor will not direct recording of minors, unrelated adults, bystanders, private conversations, private documents or screens, credentials, access codes, or security information.
9. Audio may be recorded only when the current scope includes audio and all legally required parties have authorized it.
10. The Vendor will use Reliance reports, reviews, Public controls, and evidence only for legitimate platform purposes and will not falsify or suppress evidence.
11. The Vendor has reviewed the linked Terms and Privacy Notice and can retain a copy of the exact versions shown.

## Separate affirmative acknowledgments

**Terms checkbox:** I am authorized to bind the identified Vendor and agree to the exact Reliance Terms of Use version shown.

**Privacy checkbox:** I acknowledge that I reviewed the exact Reliance Privacy Notice version shown and understand the described processing for the Vendor relationship.

**Electronic-record checkbox:** I consent to receive these Vendor terms, notices, receipts, and related records electronically and can access and retain the versions shown.

**Button:** Agree for Vendor

All required checkboxes must be selected before assent. Reliance should record each presented content key/version rather than collapse Terms, Privacy, and electronic-record evidence into an unspecified acceptance.

## Job-specific Manager attestation

When a Vendor Manager creates or materially changes a Work Record, assignment, or recording proposal, Reliance should present a separate attestation tied to that exact context:

**Exact attestation text:** I am authorized to manage this Work Record for the Vendor shown. I confirm that the assigned service professional and the job, location, recording boundary, participant roles, audio setting, purpose, and stages entered by the Vendor are accurate to the best of my knowledge. I understand that I cannot make the Customer's recording or Public choice, an Employee's job-specific Recording Participation choice, or an Employee's standing Public participation choice.

**Button:** Confirm Work Record Attestation

This attestation is an operational Vendor statement. It is not Employee consent, Customer authorization, Reliance Audit, or certification of workmanship, safety, licensure, insurance, or code compliance.

## Decline path

**Button:** I do not agree

Declining does not submit assent. Reliance should explain that the Vendor cannot use features that require this assent and provide a support path. It must not record a declined or abandoned screen as acceptance.

## Electronic-record notice

By selecting Agree for Vendor, you intend to sign electronically. Reliance should provide a durable copy or download of the exact content and record the verified principal, Vendor, role, content key, version, complete text snapshot, hash, timestamp, and request evidence. Legal sufficiency of the final electronic-record presentation remains attorney-review pending.

## Activation dependencies

- A role-specific Vendor gate that does not proxy Employee or Customer decisions.
- Versioned Terms and Privacy links plus durable-copy delivery.
- Verified organization authority and immutable assent evidence.
- Exact-context Manager attestation evidence for Work Record, assignment, and recording proposal changes.
- Material-change and fresh-assent rules.
- Decline and support behavior tested before activation.
