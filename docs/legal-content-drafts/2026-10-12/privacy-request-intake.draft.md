# Privacy Request Intake

## Introductory text

You may ask Reliance Technologies LLC about personal information associated with you. Depending on your identity, relationship, location, applicable law, and available exceptions, you may ask to access, correct, delete, or obtain information; withdraw a prospective consent; or dispute an attribution.

Use the authenticated Support & Help or privacy-request path when one is available to you. Former participants, accountless users, and anyone who cannot access that path may send a request to `colivera080124@gmail.com`. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, signed links, bearer tokens, or full payment credentials.

## Request categories

- Access or explanation
- Correction
- Deletion
- Portable copy, where applicable
- Recording or Public participation withdrawal
- Public/Private visibility assistance
- Decision or identity dispute
- SMS help or opt-out assistance
- Accessibility assistance
- Other privacy question

## Information requested from the requester

Reliance should ask only for information reasonably needed to locate the relevant records and verify identity and authority. Depending on context, that may include name, preferred contact method, role, Vendor, approximate service date, Work Record reference, and a description of the request. Reliance should not request a password, raw OTP, signed URL, or unnecessary government identifier through an ordinary form.

## Verification and authority notice

We may need to verify that you are the person concerned or are authorized to act for them before disclosing or changing information. Email address alone may not be enough for an Employee, Customer, or Vendor decision. A request submitted by an agent may require proof of authority. Reliance should offer a workable path for former or reassigned Employees without requiring membership reactivation.

## Receipt text

**Title:** Request received

We received your request and created a reference number. We will review identity, authority, applicable law, and any evidence-preservation or legal-hold requirements before acting. We may contact you for additional verification. Do not send credentials in a reply.

## Outcome text

Reliance should explain the action taken, information provided, correction recorded, deletion completed, or reason an item was retained or denied. Any exception should be described at a useful level without exposing another person's information, security controls, or privileged material. The case record should preserve the original request, verification class, actions, notices, and immutable history.

## Timing statement

Reliance will respond within the time required by applicable law for the verified request. This draft intentionally does not promise a single deadline for every requester or jurisdiction.

## Activation dependencies

- Secure intake separate from ordinary email where practical.
- Immutable case reference/event history and authorized staff workflow.
- Identity and agent verification standards.
- Data discovery, export, correction, deletion, exception, and legal-hold procedures.
- Provider request propagation where required.
- Attorney-reviewed jurisdiction and appeal handling before rights are advertised as guaranteed.
