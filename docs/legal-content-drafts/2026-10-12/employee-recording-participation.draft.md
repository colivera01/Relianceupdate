# Employee Job-Specific Recording Participation

## Display title

Your recording choice for this Service Order

## What the Employee must see

Before a decision, Reliance should display the current Service Order, Vendor, service, location or category, recording boundary, intentional participant roles, audio setting, recording purpose, and stages. The display and decision must use the same current Work Record, Vendor membership, Employee principal, assignment generation, assessment ID and generation, and scope hash.

## Core explanation

The Vendor identified you as an intentional participant in the Reliance recording request shown. This is your own job-specific choice. The Vendor, Manager, Customer, or another Employee cannot decide for you.

This choice is only about intentional recording for this Service Order and exact scope. It does not authorize Public use of your image, likeness, voice, or audio. It does not change your separate standing Public Service Video Participation choice. It does not certify workmanship, job-site safety, employment status, licensure, or code compliance.

You may allow or decline. Reliance recording remains blocked unless every required current Employee allows participation and all other Customer, Service Order, safety, location, session, and upload requirements pass. The Vendor must not retaliate or coerce you for declining an optional Reliance recording choice. Contact `colivera080124@gmail.com` if you believe someone is pressuring you to accept.

## Choice A: Allow recording participation

**Choice label:** Allow recording participation

**Exact decision text:** I allow Reliance to intentionally record me for this Work Record within the exact recording scope, boundary, participant plan, audio setting, purpose, and stages shown. This is my decision for this Service Order. It does not authorize Public use of my image, likeness, voice, or audio.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Allow

After confirmation, Reliance should truthfully show whether another required participant, Customer authorization, current Service Order release, or runtime safety gate is still pending. It must not promise Recording Ready unless the canonical server resolver passes.

## Choice B: Decline recording participation

**Choice label:** Decline recording participation

**Exact decision text:** I do not allow Reliance to intentionally record me for this Work Record and exact recording scope. Reliance recording that intentionally includes me must remain blocked. This does not automatically cancel the underlying real-world service.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Decline

The Vendor may receive a limited operational notice that recording participation was declined for the current Work Record. It should not receive unnecessary private detail and cannot override the decision.

## Safety reminder

Even after all recording choices are complete, do not record minors, unrelated adults, bystanders, unrelated private conversations, private documents or screens, credentials, access codes, or security information. If conditions change, stop and use the approved retake or revalidation path. If audio is included, do not record until all legally required parties have authorized it.

## Evidence contract

Reliance should preserve the verified Employee principal, membership, Vendor, assignment generation, Work Record, assessment ID and generation, scope hash, decision, timestamp, exact displayed text snapshot, version and hash, verified Service Order session class, and immutable evidence hash. Raw OTPs, bearer tokens, signed Service Order URLs, and passwords must not be stored as decision evidence.

## Stale and replacement contexts

An old page must fail closed after reassignment, assessment replacement, scope change, release invalidation, or membership change. A replacement Employee must make their own decision. A material scope change requires fresh participation. Historical decisions remain evidence but cannot authorize the current context.
