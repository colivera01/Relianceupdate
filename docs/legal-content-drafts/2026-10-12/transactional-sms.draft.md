# Reliance Transactional SMS Notice

## Program description

If you separately choose an available text-message option, Reliance Technologies LLC may send transactional messages about actions or records associated with your Reliance relationship. Examples include a verification code, Service Order delivery or status, account security, appointment or workflow notice, support response, or another message needed to complete a request you initiated.

Reliance does not use this consent for marketing or promotional texts. Consent to transactional SMS is optional and is not a condition of purchasing services, opening an account, accepting an assignment, or using an available email alternative.

## Delivery and security

Carriers and messaging providers are not controlled by Reliance, and delivery is not guaranteed. Do not send passwords, signed links, or other credentials by text. A one-time code may establish verified access but must not be stored as consent evidence. Reliance should preserve the opt-in version, verified number reference, timestamp, source, opt-out state, and delivery metadata needed for compliance and troubleshooting.

## Activation dependencies

- Product Owner and attorney review of the complete program, carrier registration, and consent flow.
- Confirmed provider readiness and deliverability in each enabled environment.
- Automated STOP/HELP handling, suppression, confirmation, and email fallback.
- Evidence of opt-in source/version and immutable opt-out history.
- No marketing use and no mandatory SMS gate.
- Environment capability controls that hide unavailable channels.
