# Withdrawal and Decision Dispute Notice

## Requesting help

To withdraw a prospective consent or participation choice, dispute a decision attributed to you, report coercion, or correct an identity or authority error, contact `colivera080124@gmail.com`. Do not include a password, one-time code, signed Service Order link, bearer token, or other credential.

Reliance may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity, role, authority, Vendor membership, Work Record relationship, or the decision at issue. If you no longer have an active account or Vendor membership, Reliance should provide a verified former-participant path that does not require reactivation.

## Prospective effect

A verified withdrawal applies prospectively to the authorization identified in the request. Depending on the decision and current state, Reliance should:

- block new recording that depends on withdrawn Customer authorization or Employee participation;
- block new Public publication that depends on withdrawn standing Public participation or Customer package authorization;
- remove current Public visibility from Reliance-controlled surfaces when the governing product contract requires it;
- preserve Private Proof access when it remains independently authorized;
- preserve immutable historical decisions, audit history, reports, and minimum evidence; and
- identify any legal hold, dispute, security, or other exception that prevents immediate deletion.

Withdrawal does not automatically erase historical evidence, undo processing already completed under a valid prior authorization, cancel an underlying real-world service, or retrieve copies made by others outside Reliance.

## Disputed attribution

A person may dispute that they made a decision or that the recorded identity, authority, membership, assignment, scope, or content version was correct. Reliance should create an immutable case reference, preserve the challenged evidence, record each action and outcome, and prevent unresolved disputed evidence from silently authorizing a new context where the current contract requires a hold.

A dispute is not automatically a finding that evidence is false. Reliance should distinguish the original immutable evidence from later case events and corrections rather than overwrite history.

## Coercion and retaliation

An Employee who believes a Vendor or Manager pressured or retaliated against them for a recording or Public choice may contact Reliance. Reliance should limit disclosure, preserve relevant evidence, and provide a human review. Reliance cannot adjudicate every employment claim, and this notice does not replace rights under applicable law.

## Response and closure

Reliance should acknowledge a verified request, provide a reference number, state what action was taken or what additional verification is needed, identify applicable exceptions without exposing protected information, and record closure. This draft does not promise a universal deadline; applicable legal deadlines depend on the verified request and law.

- Immutable privacy/withdrawal case history and safe staff authorization.
- Dependency resolution for recording gates and Public packages.
- Legal-hold and exception handling.
- Human review and notice templates.
- Attorney review of withdrawal scope and dispute remedies.
