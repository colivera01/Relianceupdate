# Employee Standing Public Service Video Participation

## Display title

Optional Public Service Video participation for this Vendor

## Explanation

This is an optional standing choice about future Public Service Videos associated with the Vendor shown. It is not required to accept assignments, open a Service Order, participate in Private Proof recording, or perform a service. Your Vendor or Manager cannot make or override this choice for you and must not retaliate or pressure you.

An Allow choice means Reliance may treat your image, likeness, voice, and authorized audio as eligible for Public use in an exact Service Video for this Vendor only when all of the following are also true:

- you are intentionally included and your job-specific Recording Participation for that Work Record is current;
- the Customer separately authorizes the exact completed package to be Public;
- Reliance Audit establishes Public-display eligibility;
- every other required participant permission is current;
- the exact package and media remain unchanged, active, and unrestricted; and
- the server authorizes Public visibility.

Your standing choice does not itself record you, publish anything, or authorize a Customer. It ceases to authorize new Public use when the applicable Vendor membership is inactive. Historical evidence and previously authorized visibility history remain preserved.

## Choice A: Allow standing Public participation

**Choice label:** Allow Public Service Video participation

**Exact decision text:** For my active membership with the Vendor shown, I allow Reliance to use my image, likeness, voice, and authorized audio in a Public Service Video only when I am intentionally included, I separately allowed recording participation for that exact Work Record and scope, the Customer separately chooses Share Publicly for the exact completed package, and all other Reliance Public requirements pass. This choice does not publish anything by itself. I may change it prospectively.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Allow

## Choice B: Do not allow standing Public participation

**Choice label:** Do not allow Public Service Video participation

**Exact decision text:** I do not allow new Public use of my image, likeness, voice, or audio for this Vendor membership. Reliance must keep dependent Service Videos Private or remove their controlled Public visibility as required. This choice does not prevent authorized Private Proof recording or access.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Do Not Allow

## Change and withdrawal

A verified change applies prospectively. Reliance should promptly block new dependent Public publication and remove current controlled Public visibility where the current contract requires it. Reliance cannot retrieve copies already made outside its systems. Historical decisions, evidence, and visibility history remain immutable.

## Evidence contract

Reliance should preserve the verified Employee principal, Vendor and membership, decision, sequence, timestamp, exact displayed text snapshot, version and hash, verification method, superseded evidence link, and immutable evidence hash. Email alone is not the identity boundary. A Manager cannot submit this choice.

## Activation dependencies

- Attorney review of image, likeness, voice, audio, withdrawal, and membership-cessation scope.
- Exact version/hash persistence through governed content.
- Verified Employee-only decision path with durable receipt.
- Current dependency resolution for affected Public packages.
- No employment or assignment gate tied to an Allow choice.
