# Your recording choice for this Service Order

## Recording request

- **Service Order:** [current Service Order]
- **Vendor:** [Vendor]
- **Service:** [service]
- **Location:** [location or category]
- **Recording boundary:** [boundary]
- **Intentional participant roles:** [roles]
- **Audio:** [included or not included]
- **Purpose:** [recording purpose]
- **Stages:** Starting Condition, Work in Progress, and Final Result

The Vendor identified you as an intentional participant in the Reliance recording request shown. This is your own job-specific choice. The Vendor, Manager, Customer, or another Employee cannot decide for you.

This choice is only about intentional recording for this Service Order and exact scope. It does not authorize Public use of your image, likeness, voice, or audio. It does not change your separate standing Public Service Video Participation choice. It does not certify workmanship, job-site safety, employment status, licensure, or code compliance.

You may allow or decline. Reliance recording remains blocked unless every required current Employee allows participation and all other Customer, Service Order, safety, location, session, and upload requirements pass. The Vendor must not retaliate or coerce you for declining an optional Reliance recording choice. Contact Relianceorg.support@gmail.com if you believe someone is pressuring you to accept.

## Allow recording participation

**Choice label:** Allow recording participation

**Decision text:** I allow Reliance to intentionally record me for this Work Record within the exact recording scope, boundary, participant plan, audio setting, purpose, and stages shown. This is my decision for this Service Order. It does not authorize Public use of my image, likeness, voice, or audio.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Allow

After confirmation, Reliance will show the current recording status. Recording may remain blocked while another required participant, Customer authorization, current Service Order requirement, or safety control is pending.

## Decline recording participation

**Choice label:** Decline recording participation

**Decision text:** I do not allow Reliance to intentionally record me for this Work Record and exact recording scope. Reliance recording that intentionally includes me must remain blocked. This does not automatically cancel the underlying real-world service.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Decline

The Vendor may receive a limited notice that recording participation was declined for this Work Record. The Vendor cannot override your decision.

## Safety reminder

Even after all recording choices are complete, do not record minors, unrelated adults, bystanders, unrelated private conversations, private documents or screens, credentials, access codes, or security information. If conditions change, stop and use the available retake or revalidation path. If audio is included, do not record until all legally required parties have authorized it.
