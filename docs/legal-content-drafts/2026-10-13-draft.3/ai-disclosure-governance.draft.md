# Reliance AI-Assisted Features Disclosure

Reliance may use AI-assisted tools to help summarize information, organize records, flag content for attention, suggest workflow guidance, or draft material for a person to review. AI output may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate for the circumstances.

A qualified person remains responsible for consequential decisions. AI does not make the final decision about:

- Reliance Audit or Public-display eligibility;
- Customer or Employee consent, assent, or identity;
- recording or Public visibility authorization;
- content reports, disputes, or privacy requests;
- employment, assignment, or retaliation matters;
- workmanship, job-site safety, licensure, insurance, or code compliance; or
- legal rights or professional advice.

Do not rely on AI output as legal, safety, trade, or other professional advice. Use the available correction, report, or human-support path when output appears wrong.

Reliance does not secretly repurpose information collected for service delivery, consent evidence, Private Proof, location verification, reports, or support for a materially new AI use. Reliance provides appropriate notice and obtains affirmative consent when required before a materially different use.

Do not place passwords, one-time codes, bearer tokens, full signed URLs, payment credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal information into an AI input. AI output must not be used to expose another person's private information or internal security detail.

## INTERNAL ACTIVATION NOTES - NOT USER-FACING
<!-- BEGIN INTERNAL ACTIVATION NOTES -->
- Before an AI use is enabled, document its purpose, owner, permitted inputs, provider, model/service class, human reviewer, output guard, retention, access, testing, monitoring, and rollback path.
- Minimize personal information, exclude secrets and unnecessary precise location, and preserve a risk-appropriate audit trail.
- Test factual error, unsafe instruction, discrimination, privacy leakage, prompt injection, unsupported legal conclusions, and automation bias. Never bypass server authorization or human review.
- Maintain a verified provider inventory and review provider terms, data controls, security, retention, subprocessors, and model-improvement settings.
- Do not claim that a provider never trains on, retains, or uses data unless that exact commitment is verified for the configured service.
- Require feature-specific notice, end-to-end privacy/retention/access testing, incident response, Product Owner approval, and counsel review for sensitive or high-impact uses.
