# Optional Public Service Video participation for this Vendor

This is an optional standing choice about Public Service Videos associated with the Vendor shown. It is not required to accept assignments, open a Service Order, participate in Private Proof recording, or perform a service. Your Vendor or Manager cannot make or override this choice for you and must not retaliate or pressure you.

An Allow choice means Reliance may treat your image, likeness, voice, and authorized audio as eligible for Public use in an exact Service Video for this Vendor only when all of the following are also true:

- you are intentionally included and your job-specific Recording Participation for that Work Record is current;
- the Customer separately authorizes the exact completed package to be Public;
- Reliance Audit establishes Public-display eligibility;
- every other required participant permission is current;
- the exact package and media remain unchanged, active, and unrestricted; and
- the server authorizes Public visibility.

Your standing choice does not itself record you, publish anything, or authorize a Customer.

If your Vendor membership becomes inactive, that membership no longer supplies the standing Employee authorization required for continued or new Reliance-controlled Public display that depends on this permission, unless a valid renewed or current authorization exists. After verified processing, Reliance will hold or remove dependent controlled Public display. Historical decisions and visibility evidence remain preserved. Reliance cannot retrieve copies already made outside its systems.

## Allow Public Service Video participation

**Choice label:** Allow Public Service Video participation

**Decision text:** For my active membership with the Vendor shown, I allow Reliance to use my image, likeness, voice, and authorized audio in a Public Service Video only when I am intentionally included, I separately allowed recording participation for that exact Work Record and scope, the Customer separately chooses Share Publicly for the exact completed package, and all other Reliance Public requirements pass. This choice does not publish anything by itself. I may change it prospectively.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Allow

## Do not allow Public Service Video participation

**Choice label:** Do not allow Public Service Video participation

**Decision text:** I do not allow new or continued Reliance-controlled Public use of my image, likeness, voice, or audio for this Vendor membership when that Public display depends on my standing permission. Reliance must keep dependent Service Videos Private or promptly hold or remove their controlled Public visibility after verified processing. This choice does not prevent authorized Private Proof recording or access.

**Confirmation button:** Confirm Do Not Allow

## Change or withdraw this choice

A verified change applies prospectively. Reliance promptly blocks new dependent Public publication and holds or removes current Reliance-controlled Public display that depends on a permission that has been withdrawn or has become non-current. Historical decisions, evidence, and visibility history remain immutable. Copies made outside Reliance may remain.
