# Reliance Accessibility Statement

Reliance Technologies LLC wants Customers, Vendors, service professionals, and visitors to be able to use Reliance effectively. Reliance uses WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design and engineering target for customer-facing and role-critical experiences, including keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful labels, sufficient contrast, understandable errors, responsive layouts, captions or alternatives where applicable, and compatibility with common assistive technologies.

This target is not a claim that every current page or third-party component conforms, and Reliance is not presenting itself as independently certified. Accessibility is ongoing work that includes design review, automated checks, keyboard and screen-reader testing, mobile testing, and remediation of reported barriers.

## Ask for help or report a barrier

If you cannot access a Reliance feature or document, email Relianceorg.support@gmail.com. Please describe the page or task, the barrier, your device or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing it, and a preferred way to receive help. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, signed links, or other credentials.

Reliance will review the request and provide an accessible alternative where reasonably possible. You do not need to disclose unnecessary disability or medical information.

## Language access

Reliance may provide translated content for convenience. Consequential consent or assent uses controlled, reviewed, versioned translations with a clear language identifier and exact-snapshot evidence. Machine translation alone is not treated as a verified legal translation for a consequential decision.

## Third-party content

Some third-party services or content may have separate accessibility limitations. Contact Reliance if a critical third-party component prevents access.
